# PDF recovery alternatives: readiness for initial routing

Assessment: 2026-10-28. Repository baseline: `34d88711fb529b181bb33e6d71f3a50ab40dff81`.
Research for [Assess whether PDF recovery alternatives are ready for initial routing](https://github.com/rickono/cookbook-mcp/issues/65), within [Make PDF cookbooks usable through agent-first review](https://github.com/rickono/cookbook-mcp/issues/1).

## Finding

**Keep Tesseract as the initial scan-recognition baseline.** The pinned RapidOCR PP-OCRv5 configuration is a credible targeted alternative with demonstrated sampled sandbox fit, but the existing comparison does not justify promotion. Selected retrieval, captions and fractional ingredient lines improve; complete instruction phrase checks regress and difficult table fractions remain wrong. No independent held-out complete-content comparison resolves those tradeoffs. This retains [Choose extraction and recovery routes for each PDF condition — resolution](https://github.com/rickono/cookbook-mcp/issues/6#issuecomment-6458755406), without choosing owner acceptance thresholds. [R, P1–P5]

**Docling is supported as a targeted layout/association candidate**, specifically the tested custom fixed-recognition configuration with recursive structured output. It is not a replacement recognizer, automatic recipe-ownership solution, or quantity correction authority. Identified column/order or table-association problems justify considering it; header semantics, picture children, enumeration markers and source geometry need explicit handling. Its default Markdown is unsuitable as the sole transcription. Automatic trigger accuracy has not been established. [P4]

Recognition, layout/association, automatic recipe ownership and visual correction are separate claims. None confers approval or publication eligibility. Native text remains a retained control, including on forced-OCR pages. Digital pages start with native extraction; an isolated digital omission triggers regional recovery, whereas a demonstrably unreliable scan layer motivates OCR throughout that edition without discarding native evidence. Every page still receives source review. [R]

## Scope and method

This applies the completed [Existing PDF trials against the agreed review policy](https://github.com/rickono/cookbook-mcp/blob/826d9df77f12da2a742d244611a9afe00d494a92/docs/research/pdf-review-policy-evidence-gaps.md) and its [research resolution](https://github.com/rickono/cookbook-mcp/issues/64#issuecomment-6460071554) to route selection; it does not repeat the general clearance-accuracy audit. Reports, configuration manifests, selected saved admission records and relevant source were read as text. No pixels were reopened/adjudicated, scorer rerun, PDF parsed/rendered, OCR/inference executed, dependencies provisioned, book data transferred, content approved or publication changed. The parent's current `CONTEXT.md` was consulted read-only without copying its diff or changing that checkout. Private paths below are evidence locators, not upload instructions. [A, C]

## Actual tested configurations

### Tesseract

September's matched comparison uses Linux **Tesseract 5.3.0**, **Poppler 22.12.0**, **Pillow 12.3.0**, complete composed-page JPEGs at **2300 pixels** long edge, English **PSM 3**, without production orientation/deskew. Default English traineddata SHA-256 is `7d4322bd2a7749724879683fc3912cb542f19906c83bcc1a52132556427170b2`; best-English is `8280aed0782fe27257a68ea10fe7ef324ca0f8d85bd2fd145d1c2b560bcb66ba`. Regional variants use PSM 6, white borders and optionally grayscale autocontrast. The separate direct-region diagnostic uses equivalent 2300/4600-pixel whole-page scales, with actual regions capped at 2500 pixels per edge. These configurations are distinct evidence. [P1, C]

October's direct comparator uses unchanged **2500-long-edge quality-95 JPEGs**, default English weights above, `-l eng --psm 3`, explicit TXT/TSV output, and a 90-second subprocess timeout. The launch pins image `sha256:61bd9cefc733932efaf684d9d8ad20cd7550fc4b18f2e37558a2e7c7ecfd0933`. Current production extraction is retained separately: the private control keeps native text unless it contains fewer than 40 characters after removing non-word characters, then uses the production OCR result when nonempty. That historical length heuristic is not the planned source-review routing policy. The private `run.json` includes `ocr-review-v2` metadata, but the direct arm does **not** run its orientation/deskew pipeline. Production probes four rotations at 1600 pixels, then applies projection-profile deskew (±5°, 0.25° steps) before final recognition. Confidence thresholds prioritize review; they do not certify correctness. A replacement comparison must distinguish direct recognition from this production control. [P2, C]

## Route-relevant evidence

On the October **12-page/three-edition** sample, manual recipe assembly gives RapidOCR **36/36** positive queries versus Tesseract **33/36** and current extraction **32/36**. All six cross-page targets pass with RapidOCR; all manual-boundary arms avoid recipe hits for seven valid negative controls. This supports the hypothesis that the detector recovers small/photo-caption text, not automatic boundaries or complete reading. Naive adjacent-page windows produce four wrong associations among seven negatives. [P2, P3]

Use audited scorer results: ordered RapidOCR retains **61/67** ingredient phrases versus Tesseract **45/67**, and **11/14** fractional ingredient lines versus **0/14**. Raw RapidOCR gives only **43/67**, demonstrating the ordering effect. Complete instruction phrases are **14/38** ordered RapidOCR versus **16/38** Tesseract (raw RapidOCR **6/38**); anchors are **34/38** versus **33/38**. Exact table cells improve **2/24 → 9/24**, yet every **14 fraction-bearing cell** fails both arms. Formatting/hyphenation causes some phrase failures; others are substantive. A serving-count boundary scorer amendment changes ingredient counts. The old regional arm was not rescored and cannot be ranked using mixed scorer versions. [P3]

Recognition-only cell diagnostics give v5 **5/24 → 4/24** across 300/600 dpi and v6-small **6/24 → 6/24**, with **0/14** exact fractions throughout. Crops change context and normalization, so those are not improvements over whole-page 9/24. A reference fraction remains disputed. Neither universal resolution escalation nor initial v6 promotion is supported. [P5]

Docling associates **24/24** quantities with the correct ingredient row and formulation column but marks **12/24** as headers incorrectly. Recursive JSON retains **186/186** recognized fragments; default Markdown retains **151/186**, omitting four of five photo-caption preparations on the continuation page. Default `iterate_items()` also misses picture children. An initial scorer omitted structured enumeration markers, falsely losing five fragments; marker-aware audited scoring restores them. Four-page reading checks match XY: **24/27** ingredient and **10/21** instruction phrases. Within-region ordering excludes repeated/ambiguous substrings; global authored order is not established. Layout does not recover recognition omissions or repair fraction strings. [P4]

Automatic ownership is a separate failure: primary fresh-page assembly gives **25/25** positive presence, **12/25** confined positives, **6/8** wrong negative associations and **3/6** clean complete recipe memberships. The separately enrolled continuation supplement gives **25/26**, **6/26**, **2/8**, **1/5** respectively. It joins a genuine complete final step but merges an independent preparation. Manual boundaries with the same OCR eliminate wrong negative associations. Both cohorts use previously sampled editions and builder-authored source annotations; do not pool them or credit oracle results to automation. [P6, A]

Native controls matter: September native text preserves a fractional quantity every OCR variant loses on an inspected page. Best-English whole-page scores **16/31** versus default **17/31**; manual regions **14/31**, contrast regions **19/31**, with individual regressions. Higher density fails to fix the tested fractions and loses a table probe retained at lower density. Complete PDF composition is necessary for image/mask layers; selecting one raster risks omissions. Photography does not establish absence of text. These purposive probes are not whole-content fidelity measurements. [P1]

### Later evidence through October 20

Source-supported review of three selected recipe scopes improves table checks **9/24 → 22/24** with two unresolved and instruction phrases **8/19 → 13/19**. That is correction-stage evidence, not a recognizer gain. The reference is an agent transcription with disputed glyphs, and instrumented correction time excludes setup, initial reading and evaluation. [P7]

October 12–14 whole-book proposals demonstrate richer records and continuations, but source checks expose missed quantity discrepancies and an uncorroborated reviewer replacement. Adaptive detail changes eight filled readings across 112 cells, four initially unflagged; four cells remain unresolved. Those exposed/same-context checks cannot rank OCR engines, but show why confidence-triggered recovery misses errors. A native-EPUB synthetic control matches four complete texts, 53 ingredient lines and 94 numeric occurrences under simple reflow with supplied identities; it excludes real scan complexity. [P8–P10]

October 15–20 adds a **39-page** source-first reference packet and scripted visual reading: Haiku x3 scores **528/578** pilot items and **292/293** quantity lines. Primary full-page transcription covers **859/872** pages; a separate fallback configuration covers **872/872**. This remote visual-reader pipeline is not a matched RapidOCR/Tesseract comparison. Assembler changes follow grader feedback and expose ownership regressions; v4 repairs review metadata while keeping v3 section text byte-identical. The fallback comparison has null grades. Cost/runtime telemetry is useful, while total reference/adjudication/human effort remains unmeasured. These later results do not justify OCR promotion, certify all-page correctness, or authorize repeating remote calls. [P11, A, C]

## Demonstrated sandbox fit and limits

Current admission requires the Linux VM (at most **4 GiB/two CPUs**) and verified cgroup v2 launcher: whole-job **2 GiB**, **zero swap**, **two CPUs**, **64 PIDs/threads**, **300 seconds**, one shared slot, read-only inputs/code/root and no network. Auxiliary bounds include 16 MiB per file, 128 MiB temporary filesystem and 16 MiB shared memory. `require_ocr_limits` verifies kernel limits, private cgroup namespace and live PID-1 watchdog. Environment flags, image caps, ulimits and memory polling do not replace this boundary. [S, C]

Saved October records demonstrate admission and completion for these specific configurations. RapidOCR's three matched book jobs total about **28 worker seconds**, peak **811,413,504 bytes**; matched Tesseract jobs about **45 seconds**, peak **260,218,880 bytes**. Docling's four single-page jobs total about **19.1 seconds after imports**, peak **1,254,453,248 bytes**. Recognition-only v5/v6 diagnostics peak at **159/182 MiB**. Job scopes and timing exclusions differ; these are not end-to-end throughput comparisons. Retained launch/result/container and verification records show zero swap, stopped PID zero and no OOM/PID events for successful jobs. Preserved failures include RapidOCR's synthetic enum setup error and assembly's photo-page `None` result; neither is a memory failure or proof of unreadable source. [P2–P6]

September Tesseract also ran under this boundary. Historical native macOS Paddle success does **not** demonstrate current admission or Linux fit. Remote inference is not cgroup-bounded locally; guarded rendering is a separate claim. This desk audit did not revalidate current Docker/image availability or run doctor. Saved sample fit does not establish arbitrary-page, combined-model or long-lived whole-book fit. Future jobs need fresh admission and immutable identities; failed preflight must stop rather than trigger provisioning or ceiling changes. [P1–P6, P11, S]

## One bounded comparison proposal — execution requires separate authorization

Reuse exposed evidence for development/regression controls. Do not rerun the general review-quality audit. This proposed single study addresses the unresolved route question; it selects no acceptance threshold.

1. **Freeze development and exact candidates.** Treat September probes, October matched pages, Docling/fraction diagnostics and both assembly cohorts as development only. Freeze source/image/model/configuration, worker, ordering, adapter, scorer and trigger hashes. Preserve original/audited results; adjudicate disputed references separately without rewriting history. Initial contenders: direct Tesseract 5.3.0/default English/PSM 3 and the exact whole-page RapidOCR v5 configuration above on identical composed 2500-pixel quality-95 JPEGs. Retain `ocr-review-v2` production extraction as a third control with its declared rotation/deskew settings. Score raw and frozen XY RapidOCR without new recognition. No v6/model-size/detector-size/resolution sweep.
2. **Fresh independent held-out enrollment.** Cap at **24 disjoint three-page windows / 72 unique physical pages**, from at least six editions never used for tuning. A selector without candidate exposure enrolls six windows in each stratum: clean digital/native controls; image-only or suspect-layer scans with ordinary lists; columns/formulation tables; photo/nested-caption and genuine continuation layouts. Sample randomly within preregistered eligible pools and label purposive challenge cases separately. Require genuine continuations, negative neighboring-preparation associations, a spread and layered composition in the frozen allocation. If required coverage cannot fit, report infeasibility before execution. Outer-edge truncated recipes are incomplete/unscorable for whole-recipe fidelity, but every visible region still receives scoring. If six new editions are unavailable, report the narrower feasible claim before proceeding. Reused pages never become held-out.
3. **Independent complete references.** Before candidate access, a source reader records all visible regions, full wording, parts, enumeration, quantities/units as distinct occurrences, table row/formulation, neighboring ownership and physical-page provenance. A separate adjudicator resolves disagreements; human source review covers disputes and sampled apparently correct content. Readers cannot tune candidates. Keep unreadable glyphs/associations explicit and outside exact-truth denominators, with counts reported. Preserve source-printed inconsistency separately. Freeze references privately and blind graders to arm identity. Version any amendment, apply it equally and retain first-pass results.
4. **Separate stages and triggers.** First score uncorrected recognition, retaining native text byte-for-byte wherever available. Operational digital routing stays native; shadow OCR does not authorize replacement. On layout-eligible windows compare frozen XY against the exact Docling fixed-line adapter holding RapidOCR text constant. Traverse picture children, restore structured markers, retain source line identity/transforms; assess header semantics separately from geometry and strings. This does not establish Docling with Tesseract inputs or default end-to-end integration. Use identical frozen ownership rules across recognition arms; manual boundaries are oracle diagnostics only. Freeze source-review triggers: visible omission, quantity conflict, broken order, uncertain association or missing continuation. Measure trigger misses on all pages, including unflagged/native regions. Then compare matched source-supported correction with the same reviewer/tools/context policy and blind arm assignment, reporting pre/post-review separately.
5. **Complete-content measurements.** Count region omissions, duplicates and spurious text; full recipe text, ordered ingredients, complete method streams, headnotes/parts/variations/yields; exact quantity-plus-unit occurrences with culinary owner/formulation; table geometry versus semantics; caption/enumeration retention; continuation joins, false merges/splits, independent preparation ownership, passage misclassification and citation/coordinate fidelity. Report strict comparison and separately frozen layout-normalized comparison, adjudicating formatting versus substantive errors. Anchors/paraphrase cannot substitute for complete fidelity. Supply per-edition/window/stratum denominators, incomplete/unadjudicable counts, paired wins/losses and uncertainty clustered by edition/window. Frozen positive/negative retrieval is secondary. Do not combine cells, pages, recipes and queries into one accuracy rate.
6. **Shared recovery accounting.** Each route declares one initial extraction/review, then at most **two additional attempts per problematic region and six per physical page**, shared across recognition, layout, detail and reviewers. Experimental shadow initial arms are explicit study costs, not free operational retries. Keep each route's ledger; changing tools, crop names or agents never resets it. Stable original-coordinate regions own counters; overlapping crops charge every affected region. A whole-page alternative charges the page once and every affected region once; a multi-page alternative charges each page. Failures/timeouts/unchanged retries count. A predefined preprocessing-plus-recognition run counts once; several configurations count separately. Deterministic serialization/scoring of saved outputs adds no alternative reading; new layout inference does. Stop before any counter would exceed its limit. Cap study initial jobs at 72 per direct recognizer plus 72 production-control jobs, and additional recovery runs at 432 per route; regional limits may stop sooner. Log problem, scope, evidence/config hashes, time/cost and result for every attempt. Stop early for inadequate pixels; exhausted content becomes a human edge case, technical failure leaves review pending and terminal unavailable content stays explicit. Do not import the later remote x3-voting scheme without separately agreed accounting.
7. **Resources and total effort.** Execute local heavy work sequentially through the unchanged launcher only, one page per proposed inference job, no network, already staged immutable images/weights. Retain doctor/launch/watchdog/kernel peak/event, exit and cleanup evidence; record PID/thread use and CPU throttling. Separate admission, cold initialization, rendering, inference, ordering, layout, recovery, scoring, review and end-to-end times. Count all failed/missing units. Do not co-load models based on separate fit evidence. Time human reference preparation, source inspection, correction, uncertainty decisions, adjudication and audit, including unflagged checks; distinguish agent from owner effort. Compare matched correct content and total effort, not engine speed alone. No paid/remote arm is proposed.
8. **Evidence needed for promotion.** Before held-out access the owner must settle acceptance unit, acceptable regression/error margins, unresolved visibility and quality/effort tradeoffs through [Define usable-content acceptance and missed-error validation](https://github.com/rickono/cookbook-mcp/issues/5). A separately reviewed immutable paired result must meet those criteria on fresh complete scopes, retain native advantages, avoid unacceptable instruction/quantity/ownership regressions and demonstrate resource/budget fit and justified recovery/effort benefits. Stratum-specific promotion needs its own evidence; aggregate gains cannot hide losses. Docling additionally needs measured trigger sensitivity/false positives, structured retention, association benefits and header-error handling. Held-out-driven tuning creates a new candidate needing a new holdout. Failure or insufficient precision leaves Tesseract initial. A successful research comparison still needs a route decision and implementation review; it grants no approval/publication authority.

## Questions sharpened for the parent

- Which acceptance units/regression margins govern ingredient gains alongside worse instruction fidelity?
- Who can prepare and adjudicate fresh complete references without candidate exposure, and what total human effort is feasible?
- Is the candidate direct v5 plus frozen XY, or a future adapter with orientation handling? A different adapter does not inherit these scores.
- What source-review evidence triggers Docling, and how do uncertain ownership/header semantics remain visible afterward?
- Can a separately authorized study enroll enough new editions and complete continuation scopes within this cap; if not, which narrower routing claim is useful?

No follow-up tickets or map edits were made. Owner policy, execution authorization and pilot selection remain with the parent.
