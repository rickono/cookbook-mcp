## Consequences for the specification and repair plan

Freeze a cohort by edition hash, published candidate digest, publication identity and processing revision. Include all published PDFs and known local/held candidates, while preserving their different access and review states. Reuse retained outputs as comparison baselines; source-image inspection and independent complete-content checks must establish which candidate is better. Counts, nonempty text, payload integrity and historical activation receipts cannot supply fidelity rates or measured acceptance criteria.

Before acceptance measurement, resolve current access, source-image coverage, raw-response completeness, prior exposure and independently reviewed reference scopes. Local EPUB discovery found no exact-title format matches for these inventoried editions; that narrow result does not exclude differently titled matching works. No edition-aligned EPUB alternative was established. Legitimate publisher/author alternatives belong to the companion transcript-source research; this inventory authorizes neither acquisition nor reuse.

The general alternate-reader operating policy remains a human decision. Historical blanket refusal text is not reinstated as a research blocker: the owner authorized one local Luna retry, which returned text. That fact establishes neither reviewed correctness nor broader recovery/publication authority. This audit supplies no provider comparison or newly measured error rate. OpenAI Decisions and Jev remain for the later authorized controlled comparison. [Owner scope and policy context](https://github.com/rickono/cookbook-mcp/issues/110).
